Faster embedded SRT fallback after a decoder retry.

- If playback restarts with MKV range reading disabled, download the Plex SRT sidecar alongside video startup instead of waiting for the new decoder-ready gate.
- When **Settings → About → Enable diagnostics** is on, the on-screen panel identifies whether subtitles came from MKV byte ranges or Plex's sidecar, including the reason an MKV read failed. This makes a zero-range readout actionable.
- Diagnostics remain completely inactive when the setting is off.
