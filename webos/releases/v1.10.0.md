# Plezy webOS v1.10.0

- Direct Play compatible Plex MKV and MP4 files with H.264 or HEVC instead of unnecessarily transcoding them.
- Recognize supported AC3/EAC3 and additional HD Matroska audio formats, with webOS 4.x bitrate and codec-profile limits.
- Preserve subtitle and audio-track selection by using HLS when the TV cannot honor the selected embedded tracks.
- Automatically retry Plex HLS when the LG decoder rejects a Direct Play source, keeping fast native resume support.
- Add codec-selection, source MIME, decoder-fallback and Plex request regression tests.

Actual LG C8 Direct Play compatibility and latency improvements still require physical TV verification.
