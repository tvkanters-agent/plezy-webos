Split Enhanced playback into two independent options under Settings → Video Playback → Player:

- **Enhanced SRT playback** enables Direct Play and local PixiJS rendering for SRT/SubRip.
- **Enhanced ASS playback** enables Direct Play and local libass rendering for ASS/SSA.

Each switch defaults off unless the former Enhanced playback setting was enabled, in which case both are initially enabled. A disabled format uses the original Plex compatibility playback with server-side subtitle burn-in, without initializing either subtitle renderer. Selection happens before opening video or requesting subtitle data. Other or disabled subtitle formats use compatibility playback as well.

Switching between locally rendered SRT and ASS changes only the overlay; it does not restart the video. Switching to or from Plex burn-in necessarily selects a new video stream. Subtitle sync is unavailable when the active subtitle is burned in or off. The existing native resume start timestamp is unchanged.

The new routes and switching behavior are covered by unit and browser tests; subtitle pixels and playback on LG hardware still require TV verification.
