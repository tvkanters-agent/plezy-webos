# Plezy webOS 1.15.0

## Playback compatibility toggle

- Added **Enhanced playback (Direct Play + subtitle renderers)** at the very top of Settings. It defaults to **Off** for existing and new installations.
- **Off** restores the original Plex playback pipeline from before the Direct Play and local subtitle changes: HLS with Plex burned-in subtitles for MKV and other incompatible formats, without independent subtitle extraction or extra transcoder decisions. Compatible MP4 playback retains the historical direct path.
- **On** uses the newer guarded Direct Play and local SRT/ASS subtitle renderers, including their automatic fallback to Plex burn-in.
- Subtitle timing is greyed out in compatibility mode, remains selectable by remote, and explains that Enhanced playback must be enabled in Settings.
- The selected mode is saved on the TV and takes effect the next time playback opens or restarts.

The legacy and enhanced request paths are covered by automated tests. Physical LG TV playback and visible burned-in subtitles still need confirmation on the affected server.
