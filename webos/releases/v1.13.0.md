# Plezy webOS 1.13.0

- Open **Audio & Subtitles** in the More options menu of a movie, show, season or episode before starting playback.
- A movie remembers its chosen tracks for its selected media version. Shows and seasons select tracks on the episode their Play button would start, and carry matching language/track preferences to subsequent episodes.
- Track selection leaves the picker open for adjusting both columns. No Plex stream changes or playback start until you press Play; the existing TV Back/focus behavior is preserved.
- Add browser regressions for movie version/stream IDs and show/season preferences.

GitHub Actions verifies the Chromium 53 bundle, browser behavior and installable IPK. Actual LG TV decoder and remote behavior remain hardware verification items.
