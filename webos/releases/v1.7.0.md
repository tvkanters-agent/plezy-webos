# Plezy webOS v1.7.0

- Audio and subtitle selections made from the player's TrackSheet or an episode's More options menu now carry to other episodes of the same series, including different seasons, whenever an equivalent source track exists.
- Matching follows Plezy's language and track semantics: original versus commentary audio, forced subtitles, and explicit subtitles Off are preserved. Missing or ambiguous matches use the server's per-episode defaults.
- Episode stream details are fetched as needed before playback, so choices made from season-list episodes reach the right stream IDs.
- Preferences last for the current app session and are isolated per series/server; this does not bulk-edit every Plex episode.

Live Plex transcoding and LG webOS 4.4.3 hardware verification remain necessary.
