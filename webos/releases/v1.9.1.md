# Plezy webOS v1.9.1

Fixes the broken subtitle-sync UI and TV remote navigation introduced in v1.9.0.

- Port Plezy's top-centered compact subtitle-sync bar, with a settings row that opens the bar and focuses the slider automatically.
- Left/right adjust by 50 ms; Up/Down exit the slider directly to the −/+ buttons without altering its value. OK moves focus to Close; every button, Reset, and Back work with the LG remote.
- Keep Plezy's ±10 second fine slider, allow ±60 seconds with the step buttons, and accelerate long presses.
- Match Plezy's signed `ms`/`s` labels and visible slider-thumb focus treatment.
- Verify navigation, focus, long press, offset limits, and live subtitle cues in Playwright.

Native subtitle rendering on a physical LG webOS TV still requires device verification.
