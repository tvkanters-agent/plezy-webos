# Plezy webOS 1.4.0

- **Persistent libraries:** Previously loaded library titles and Plex alphabet indexes reappear from local storage, including after restarting the app.
- **Live updates:** Each library visit also requests fresh metadata. Changed titles, artwork revisions and recommendations are updated on the visible page without blanking it; unchanged content is left alone.
- **Home remains fresh:** Home shelves are never persisted and are fetched from the server on every new app session, retaining short-lived in-session caching and playback/watch-state invalidation.
- **Resilient and bounded:** Offline library snapshots remain usable when refresh fails. Data is isolated by server and account, cleared on connection removal, and limited to 20 MiB on disk and 10 MiB in memory.
- **Navigation fidelity:** Keep artwork's first-frame cache, remote focus, scroll position and Plex/Jellyfin alphabet behavior.
- CI verifies unit tests, end-to-end browser navigation (including cold starts and revalidation), Chromium 53 compatibility, and IPK packaging. Actual LG hardware timings still require device measurements.
