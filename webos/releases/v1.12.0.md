# Plezy webOS 1.12.0

Restore native Direct Play with a stricter compatibility gate and reliable startup recovery.

- Direct Play eligible AVC/HEVC MP4/MKV files on native webOS, avoiding unnecessary video transcoding.
- Validate actual stream codecs, profiles, levels, frame rates, chroma and audio constraints against the target C8/webOS 4.0 limits. Multiple-audio and uncertain/incompatible expanded sources retain Plex HLS.
- Keep recovery armed after metadata until playback advances; automatically retry native rejection or startup hangs through HLS.
- Report HLS failures correctly even while an earlier Play promise remains unresolved.
- Retain selected version, resume position, audio/subtitle IDs and external SRT/ASS rendering through recovery.

[Full review and validation limits](https://github.com/tvkanters-agent/plezy-webos/blob/v1.12.0/webos/DIRECT_PLAY_REVIEW.md).

Automated tests exercise compatibility policy, native source configuration and browser recovery. Physical LG C8 playback remains unverified; these checks do not establish hardware codec compatibility or identify the exact cause of the previously reported intermittent failure.
