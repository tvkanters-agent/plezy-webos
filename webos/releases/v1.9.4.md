# Plezy webOS v1.9.4

Fix playback not automatically resuming after confirmed seeks and correct subtitle-sync UI/focus on LG TVs.

- Seek-preview movement no longer pauses the video or sends a decoder seek. OK commits a single seek.
- Automatically resume when previously playing, including when old webOS hardware delays or never emits seek completion until playback is restarted. Intentionally paused playback stays paused.
- Subtitle sync controls: **Up/Down adjusts ±50ms**; **Left/Right navigates all buttons and the slider** without changing the value.
- Replace fragile Unicode symbols with SVGs for −, +, Reset and Close, matching Plezy's compact control styling.
- Add browser regressions that check confirmed seeking, delayed/stuck decoder events, full remote navigation, and graphical controls.

LG 4.x hardware validation remains needed for actual decoder timing and TV font/compositing behavior.
