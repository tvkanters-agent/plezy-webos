# Plezy webOS v1.8.1

- Keep Right-button navigation within the currently focused Home carousel. At its final card, pressing Right leaves focus there instead of jumping to another carousel and scrolling it.
- Continue to support normal movement between cards, vertical navigation between carousels, and Left to the sidebar from the first card.

Automated browser regression tests cover the behavior with carousels of different lengths. Physical LG webOS remote verification remains outstanding.
