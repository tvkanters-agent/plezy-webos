Remove the Chromium 53 ASS dithering workaround introduced in 1.19.6. On the LG C8 it added a visible pattern without correcting the sign colors. Local ASS rendering again uses smooth transparency.

With About → Enable diagnostics on, pausing on an ASS sign shows a comparison in the upper right. It combines the current video frame and the local ASS canvas into a single opaque image on the TV. A black or magenta video background indicates the decoder may not expose the frame to the web app.
