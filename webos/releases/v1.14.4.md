# Plezy webOS 1.14.4

- If local subtitle extraction or rendering fails, retry the selected subtitle with Plex burn-in automatically, including when both MKV byte-range reads and the standalone subtitle endpoint fail. The switch is silent and preserves the current playback position.
- Detect empty/unreadable text subtitles and ASS renderers that fail to initialize, rather than leaving captions invisible without retrying.
- Grey out subtitle timing controls when the video has burned-in subtitles. The row remains focusable and displays **Cannot offset burned-in subtitles** when selected.
