# Plezy webOS v1.4.1

- Home opens instantly on same-session navigation by restoring its existing rendered content and artwork, not rebuilding it. The 45-second Home freshness window is retained through background revalidation.
- Re-selecting the current Home or library destination no longer unnecessarily blanks/rebuilds it.
- Cached and uncached library, recommended, Home and alphabet content is displayed immediately when ready. When it is not ready until after an empty frame, it fades in instead of appearing as a black blink.
- Cached Series / library content remains visible during background refresh and preserves existing artwork behavior, pagination, and focus.
- Unit and browser tests cover warm navigation, repeated Home restoration, Back, short-delay fades and the existing playback, alphabet and artwork functionality. Tested by CI; physical LG TV speed was not benchmarked.
