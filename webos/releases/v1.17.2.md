Fixes SRT/VTT border rendering introduced in v1.17.1:

- Replaces the WebKit text stroke, which covered the subtitle fill and produced sharp spikes at W/V joins, with rounded circular outlines painted behind the text.
- The Border Size setting continues to scale outline thickness from 0 (off) to 5 while preserving the foreground letter shapes and making the outline even in all directions.
- Adds a browser-level pixel regression test checking that applying the outline does not erase the subtitle's solid colour, plus geometric tests for circular outlines.

Settings controls, ASS/SSA rendering and legacy Plex burn-in are unchanged. Automated Chromium rendering has been inspected; physical LG TV verification remains outstanding.
