# Plezy webOS v1.10.2

Restore reliable Plex playback after reports of intermittent video failures.

- Roll back v1.10.0's expanded native HEVC/MKV Direct Play selection. These files return to the previously working Plex HLS transcode path.
- Keep the established H.264/AAC/MP3 MP4/M4V/MOV Direct Play handling.
- Preserve v1.10.1's independent seek auto-resume and subtitle-sync control fixes.

LG TV playback remains the final compatibility test. Any future expansion of native formats should be validated on the target webOS 4.4 hardware before release.
