# Plezy webOS v1.6.0

- The player now offers Plezy-style audio and subtitle selection together, with language/codec descriptions and the currently selected tracks marked.
- Episode details and episode rows on season pages now have a More options menu. You can mark an episode watched or unwatched and select its audio/subtitle streams.
- Track choices apply to that episode's next playback, including the correct Plex source stream IDs. Missing season-list stream metadata is fetched on demand.
- TV remote navigation, menu dismissal and selected-item focus are covered by browser tests.

Physical LG webOS 4.4.3 and live Plex transcoder verification remain necessary.
