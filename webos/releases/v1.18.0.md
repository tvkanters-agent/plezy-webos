Added optional on-screen diagnostics for embedded SRT subtitles.

- Find **Subtitle diagnostics** in **Settings → Video Playback → Subtitles**. It is **off by default**.
- When enabled, playback of embedded SRT in MKV shows a readable timing panel for the video startup, MKV index, first rendered subtitle and Plex byte-range requests. Photograph the TV screen to share the timings—no browser inspector or additional software needed.
- When disabled, no diagnostic measurements, callbacks or display are created. Switching the setting takes effect on the next playback.
- The earlier inspector-dependent console logging remains removed.
