Removes the embedded SRT timing instrumentation introduced in v1.17.5:

- No more startup timing console messages or per-range measurement bookkeeping.
- Removes diagnostic callback plumbing and the corresponding tests.
- Preserves subtitle extraction, player startup behavior, rendering, and the SRT border improvements in v1.17.6.

The old webOS Web Inspector proved impractical for retrieving diagnostics on the target TV. No further tooling or inspector setup is required for this release.
