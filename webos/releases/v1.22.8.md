Seeking from a paused video now automatically starts playback once the seek has buffered. This applies to confirmed remote timeline seeks and direct timeline/chapter jumps; LG playback recovery continues retrying until the decoder advances.

Pressing Pause during the seek or buffering cancels automatic playback. Canceling an unconfirmed remote seek preview still leaves an originally paused video paused.

Includes unit and browser regression tests. Physical LG webOS decoder behavior should be verified on a TV.
