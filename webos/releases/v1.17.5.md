Adds safe startup timing diagnostics for slow embedded MKV SRT subtitles:

- Logs the delay before subtitle extraction starts, MKV index discovery, first cue extraction, and rendering.
- Reports byte-range request count, transferred bytes and cumulative request duration.
- Distinguishes local MKV extraction from the Plex subtitle fallback, without exposing Plex URLs or tokens.

The previous first-cue optimization produced no noticeable change on the target LG TV. This release makes it possible to identify the actual bottleneck before modifying the playback pipeline again. Playback and subtitle rendering behavior are unchanged.
