# Plezy webOS 1.2.6

- Fix cast carousel artwork briefly displaying square corners during its entry/fade animation on Chromium 53.
- Centralize image-level rounded clipping in the common artwork renderer. All artwork now inherits its containing frame's exact corner radius, protecting future artwork surfaces against the same compositor clipping issue.
- Preserve circular actor portraits, rounded poster thumbnails and episode art, and transparent detail backdrops.
- Add regression coverage for cast carousel corners, actor portraits and generic artwork frames to prevent regressions.
