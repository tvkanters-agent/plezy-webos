# Plezy webOS v1.8.3

- Skip Intro/Credits now uses Plezy's rounded, filled focus appearance with subtle scaling, without the ugly external square outline.
- After selecting Skip Intro, the button disappears immediately and stays hidden through seeking and buffering, even if LG temporarily reports the original playback time. Explicitly seeking back into that section makes the prompt available again.
- Automatic skipping and other markers retain their independent behavior.

Automated tests simulate delayed decoder seeking and LG remote focus, including a subsequent manual rewind. Physical LG webOS hardware verification is still pending.
